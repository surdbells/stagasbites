Nigerian food in Oakville & the GTA

A taste of Lagos, made in Oakville

Staga’s Bites is a Nigerian kitchen in Oakville, Ontario specialising in small chops, hand-made pastries and flame-grilled suya, asun and chicken. Everything is prepared by a certified food handler and cooked fresh for your pickup or delivery slot.

We serve Oakville, Mississauga, Burlington, Milton and the wider Greater Toronto Area, whether you need a dozen meat pies for the weekend or full event catering for a few hundred guests.
